4. Intended meaning of the subtag:

Uzbek text written in the reformed Latin-based orthography established by
Law No. ZRU-1184 of 21 October 2026, in force from 22 April 2027: 28
letters and the tutuq belgisi (U+02BC). Compared with the orthography
tagged '1995', oʻ, gʻ, sh and ch are replaced by ö, ğ, ş and ç, and ng is
no longer a separate unit of the alphabet. The alphabet (Article 1 of Law
No. 931-XII in the wording of Law No. ZRU-1184, version in force from 22
April 2027):

A a, B b, D d, E e, F f, G g, Ğ ğ, H h, I i, J j, K k, L l, M m, N n, O o,
Ö ö, P p, Q q, R r, S s, Ş ş, Ç ç, T t, U u, V v, X x, Y y, Z z, and the
tutuq belgisi ʼ (U+02BC).

6. Any other relevant information:

Native name: Lotin yozuviga asoslangan oʻzbek alifbosi (2026).
The law was officially published on 21 October 2026 and takes effect on 22
April 2027. Documents, currency and securities issued with the earlier
letter forms remain valid, and existing state symbols and signs may stay in
use until they wear out or until a date set by the government's
implementation programme. Both Latin orthographies will therefore remain in
use side by side. The subtag is not to be used together with '1995'.

Orthographic rules for the 2026 alphabet have not yet been adopted. We can
provide further references on request.
