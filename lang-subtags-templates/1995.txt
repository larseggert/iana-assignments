4. Intended meaning of the subtag:

Uzbek text written in the Latin-based orthography established by Law No.
71-I of 6 May 1995 and in official use in Uzbekistan until 22 April 2027.
The alphabet (Article 1 of Law No. 931-XII as amended by Law No. 71-I): 26
letters, the letter combinations sh, ch and ng, and the tutuq belgisi
(apostrophe):

A a, B b, D d, E e, F f, G g, H h, I i, J j, K k, L l, M m, N n, O o, P p,
Q q, R r, S s, T t, U u, V v, X x, Y y, Z z, Oʻ oʻ, Gʻ gʻ, Sh sh, Ch ch, Ng
ng, and the tutuq belgisi ʼ.

Spelling follows the Main Orthographic Rules of the Uzbek Language approved
by Resolution of the Cabinet of Ministers No. 339 of 24 August 1995. The
subtag is intended for the large body of existing Latin-script Uzbek text
(legislation, education, media, the web) and for text that keeps this
orthography after the reform.
