ptype        Definition                                              Description
body   [RFC 8601, Section 2.3] The property being reported was found in the body of the message.
dns    [RFC 8904]              The property being reported belongs to the Domain Name System.
header [RFC 8601, Section 2.3] The property being reported was found in a header field of the message.
policy [RFC 8601, Section 2.3] The property being reported relates to a locally-defined policy.
smtp   [RFC 8601, Section 2.3] The property being reported is a parameter to an SMTP command used to relay the message.
